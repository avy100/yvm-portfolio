Custom pieces

Make it yours

  • Reworked jeansCut apart and rebuilt into a new shape: wide leg, bias skirt, corset top.
  • PatchworkPanels from several old pairs pieced into one garment.
  • Hand embroideryWaves, shells and lettering stitched by hand onto pockets, hems or yokes.
  • Bleach and paintHand-painted designs, bleach washes and custom fading.
  • Ruffles and laceOrganza or lace trims added to hems, cuffs and seams.
Repairs

Keep them going

  • Visible mendingSashiko-style running stitch over a patch, so the repair becomes a feature.
  • Invisible darningThread rewoven into thin spots and small holes in matching colour.
  • Crotch and thigh blowoutsReinforced from the inside before the hole spreads.
  • Knees and pocketsTorn knees backed or patched, pocket bags replaced.
  • Hems and hardwareOriginal-hem shortening, new zips, buttons and rivets.
